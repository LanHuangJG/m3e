<p> M3E applies the <a href="https://m3.material.io/styles/typography" target="_blank">Material Design 3 typography system</a> to encode visual hierarchy, editorial tone, and spatial rhythm across components. The system defines a <strong>30-style type scale</strong>—comprising 15 baseline and 15 emphasized roles. </p>
<p> Typography tokens follow the prefix <code>--md-sys-typescale-&#42;</code> for the <strong>standard</strong> typescale and <code>--md-sys-typescale-emphasized-&#42;</code> for <strong>emphasized</strong>. Each typescale variant encodes font size, line height, weight, and tracking (or letter spacing). </p>
<p> For example, <code>body-large</code> is encoded using <code>--md-sys-typescale-body-large-font-size</code>, <code>--md-sys-typescale-body-large-font-weight</code>, <code>--md-sys-typescale-body-large-line-height</code>, and <code>--md-sys-typescale-body-large-tracking</code>. </p>
<p> M3E components support the full semantic range of the Material 3 type system, including <strong>standard</strong> and <strong>emphasized</strong> variants of: <code>display-large</code>, <code>display-medium</code>, <code>display-small</code>, <code>headline-large</code>, <code>headline-medium</code>, <code>headline-small</code>, <code>title-large</code>, <code>title-medium</code>, <code>title-small</code>, <code>body-large</code>, <code>body-medium</code>, <code>body-small</code>, <code>label-large</code>, <code>label-medium</code>, and <code>label-small</code>. </p>
<p> Each role is mapped to a tokenized style and applied contextually—for example, <code>label-large</code> for buttons, <code>body-large</code> for list items, and <code>body-small</code> for form-field hints. </p>
